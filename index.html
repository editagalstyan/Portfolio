About Me

"Design is intelligence made visible through monochromatic precision."

I am Edita Galstyan, a passionate Graphic Designer committed to visual clarity, minimalism, and timeless editorial styling. My work spans brand identity design, print publications, custom sticker series, and analytical infographics.

By stripping away unnecessary visual noise, I bring focus to grid symmetry, striking typography, and high-contrast compositions that communicate ideas effectively.
